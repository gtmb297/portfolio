How I work with Product and Sales

Positioning and competitive strategy

I define where a product fits in the buyer's stack and why it deserves budget. At Unosecur, I position Unified Identity Fabric across human identities, non-human identities and AI agents. I also run competitive research and brief analysts.

Launch and adoption

I work with Product and Engineering on what a release means for the buyer, then translate it into messaging, demos and lifecycle communications. My work spans Ark AI at Unosecur and Access Reviews at Scrut.

Sales enablement and customer proof

I build enterprise first-call decks, competitive battlecards and business cases. I use Sales feedback to sharpen the pitch and work with sellers to develop customer stories and testimonials.

Demand and market education

I build tools, campaigns and executive perspectives around questions buyers already ask. At Scrut I led Compliance Compass. At Unosecur I lead integrated campaigns around security events and shape founder thought leadership.
